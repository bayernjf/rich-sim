## 主帖（通用版 · 英文，适合 HN / 独立开发社区）

**Title: Show people what wealth actually costs, then let them run the math on their own income**

> See the real holding cost of being rich first — then do the math with your own salary.

rich-sim is a financial-literacy simulation, not an advisor. Two tracks:

1. **Play rich.** Pick an identity card (new-money worker or old-money heir) and walk through a year of bills: the $1.3M annual holding cost, a 30-year fast-forward, a black-swan income halving, leverage at 2× cashflow (see it break at 4.35×), a fully-leveraged 6× acquisition (negotiation floor 2.44×), random events (lawsuit / crisis / family split), a shopping cart where every item lands on next year's bill, and an exchange-rate time machine showing $1M's value across history.

2. **Measure yourself.** Carry the simulated bills over to your real income and expense and get the sufficiency line, the gap, and the years — with first-class states: reachable / unreachable / no net savings.

Rules that matter: every number is arithmetic on your own assumptions, all illustrative multiples are labeled as such, no recommendations, no financial products, no predictions — and no user financial data ever leaves the device unless you sign in and sync it yourself.

26 interactive mechanics, bilingual, PWA, no account needed.

https://app.rich-sim.bayjf.com

## 中文社区版（V2EX / 即刻 · 口语化）

**标题：做了一个「财富模拟」：先让你玩一把富豪，再用你自己的收入算账**

做了个小工具，想分享给同样对「钱到底怎么算」好奇的人。

**一句话**：先看见，再算清——先玩一把富豪，看清财富的真实持有成本；再用你自己的收入，算出「够用线 / 差距 / 年限」。

**好玩的部分**：
- 两张身份卡开局（新钱打工人 / 老钱继承人），年账单 $1.3M 摆在你面前
- 人生快进 30 年、黑天鹅收入腰斩、加杠杆 2×（超过 4.35× 利息吃光现金流）、6× 全杠杆收购（谈判底价 2.44×）、诉讼/危机/分产事件卡、汇率时间机（$1M 在不同历史日期值多少）
- 购物车里的每件商品都会变成下一年的账单——负担率实时变色
- 一键把模拟账单搬进你自己的收支，算够用线、差距和年限

**原则**：只对你自己填的假设做算术；倍数和参数都标注为教学示意；不荐股、不推荐产品、不承诺收益；不登录也能全程用。

**体验**：https://app.rich-sim.bayjf.com
